High-Probability Interview Q&A (Click to Reveal)
Q1: Why use a paired t-test over an independent two-sample t-test?
Answer: In our evaluation harness, the exact same held-out test prompts are evaluated by both Treatment ($T$) and Control ($C$). Because the inputs are identical, query-level difficulty acts as a confounder. A paired $t$-test evaluates differences $D_i = S_{T,i} - S_{C,i}$, eliminating query-to-query variance and drastically increasing statistical power ($\text{Var}(\bar{D}) = \frac{\sigma_T^2 + \sigma_C^2 - 2\text{Cov}(T,C)}{N}$).
Q2: How does Matryoshka Embedding (256d) achieve a 65% latency cut?
Answer: Matryoshka Representation Learning (MRL) enforces nested vector spaces during training. By truncating 1536-dim embeddings down to 256-dim, initial Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) search over 5M vectors requires 83% less memory bandwidth and distance computations. Top 30 candidates are then passed to Cohere Rerank v3, preserving high precision ($NDCG@10 = 0.952$) while cutting query times from 120ms to 42ms.
Q3: How do you prevent false-positive rollbacks in automated drift detection?
Answer: We use Population Stability Index (PSI) with a multi-window confirmation logic. A single transient spike ($PSI > 0.10$) triggers a warning state, but an automated rollback is only dispatched if $PSI > 0.10$ persists for 3 consecutive 5-minute windows or if guardrail faithfulness falls below 0.90, preventing unnecessary circuit-breaker trips.

Modus AI Architecture Specifications & Engineering Whitepaper

1. Executive Summary

Modus AI brings production-grade A/B testing discipline—modeled after LinkedIn’s XLNT platform—to Generative AI systems. Replacing subjective "does this look better" evaluation with statistically rigorous ship/no-ship decisions, real-time DLT telemetry, and automated zero-downtime rollback capabilities.

2. Four Core Infrastructure Pillars

  • Controlled Experimentation for Ship Decisions: Treatment-vs-control evaluation harness scoring RAG changes against a held-out judge council (faithfulness, context recall, coherence). Paired t-tests dictate ship/no-ship decisions.
  • Guardrail Metrics & Real-Time Monitoring: Trace-level telemetry streamed via Delta Live Tables into Delta Lake to power live guardrail dashboard. Flags retrieval/latency regressions instantly.
  • Winning-Variant Selection at Scale: Multi-million row catalog benchmarking across Dense Vector, Sparse BM25, and Hybrid strategies. Matryoshka reduced embeddings + Cohere Rerank cut query times by 65%.
  • Decision Automation & Governance: Automated drift detection triggering instant rollbacks governed under Databricks Unity Catalog behind a rate-limited FastAPI gateway.
